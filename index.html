The Night Begins

Luna Novella 15. Luna Press Publishing, 7 February 2023. Horror.

Freshman Darcy Mills wants nothing more than to repair a strained relationship with her mother. When Darcy receives a letter from Althea asking for help with a downsizing move to Dallas, she is excited at the prospect of reconnecting.

Facing the frightening memories trapped in the walls of her childhood home is daunting, but Darcy is determined to have Althea back. Unfortunately, the past isn't the only thing to haunt Darcy. An evil lurks in the nearby woods, and it has noted her arrival.

Maryneal, 1962

Wild Ink Publishing, June 2025. Coming of age horror.

Warned by a soothsayer at a travelling fair that something evil is about to come to Maryneal, sixteen-year-old Delah Nix chalks it up to carnie theatrics and ignores the woman's suggestion to leave before dark. Then her sister is savagely attacked on the way home, and the murder leaves the small farming community stunned.

Delah starts to suspect something supernatural when a childhood friend, bitten trying to save her sister, gains powers and a thirst for the county's livestock. With the full moon approaching and no cure in sight, she may have to kill the boy next door.

An American Werewolf in London meets American Graffiti.

About Abigail

Abigail F. Taylor is an award winning author of Indigenous-Irish descent, best known for her slice of life horror novels and short stories, often with historical settings and always inspired by her heritage. She has been known to dabble with romance.

In a past life she was a horse girl, a theatre kid, and worked in the indie horror film spaces. When she's not writing she is out in nature, practising aikido or cross stitching, with four cats, a small dog and a sassy rooster. All Texan.
